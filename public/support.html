Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a job?
Simply go to the Jobs page, search or filter for roles that match your skills, click on a job to view the details, and hit "Apply on Employer Site". You can also send us a WhatsApp message with the job title and we'll fast-track your application.
Is Vertex Talent free for job seekers?
Yes, 100% free. We never charge candidates for finding them a role. Our fees are paid by employers only.
How long does it take to get matched?
Most candidates receive their first interview offer within 5–10 working days, depending on the role, industry, and your availability. Our fastest placement was within 48 hours!
Can you help with CV writing?
Absolutely. We offer free CV reviews and optimisation. Send your CV to hello@vertextalent.co.uk or upload it via your profile and our specialists will get back within 24 hours.
Do you place workers outside the UK?
We specialise in UK placements but also support international candidates relocating to the UK. Contact us on WhatsApp for details.
How do I reset my PIN?
Click "Forgot PIN?" on the login page, or message us on WhatsApp for a secure reset link. We'll verify your identity and get you back in within minutes.
